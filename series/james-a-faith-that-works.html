James : A Faith That Works

James 1–5 · 11 sermons · September–November 2025

Eleven messages move through James from faith tested under pressure to a church that prays, restores wanderers, and actively cares for one another. James repeatedly tests Christian claims by visible obedience: perseverance, impartial mercy, living faith, disciplined speech, heavenly wisdom, humble dependence, patient waiting, and practical service.
