Overview

  1. 00:51 Only God sets a heart on fire

    Scripture: 2 Corinthians 4:6

    • One of the most significant moments of Josh’s life. Church brat, three generations of preachers in the Wyatt family; he swore he would never do it. Pew, piano, hypocrisy. He held rebellion until 23. Nothing the world offers comes close to what God made known in Christ.
    • 2 Corinthians 4: God who said let light shine out of darkness made his light shine in our hearts. Knowledge of glory had been jammed into his mind—sermons, studies, baptism, songs—until light shone in the heart. Unshakable thirst to spread that knowledge. Gymnasium at Fort Lewis College in Durango. Man can shape or discourage; only God sets a heart on fire. If man convinced him into ministry, man would have convinced him out.
    • The call is not the microphone, authorship, or notoriety. He will tell you what he thinks. He wants to handle the Word passionately and walk with humble transparency that comes with a new heart. Jesus washed the past, gives joy in the present, hope for the future. Subpar speaker who studies like a madman. Called to preach Christ crucified, holes in his body, walking out of a borrowed tomb. Restless for those who do not know the risen Christ reigns and will judge. Glorified wedding planner getting the bride ready.

    1. 05:49 Six weeks in Ezekiel: look at the unseen

      Scripture: Romans 12:1 Colossians 1:16

      • Whatever voices you heard this week, come in and be renewed. Presenting your life as a living sacrifice is always the best move. As they begin a six-week series in Ezekiel, the goal is simple: remind you of the unseen. Stop letting the visible inform everything.
      • Colossians 1: in him all things were created, visible and invisible. Two worlds, same Creator. Visible we know; invisible is mystery. Some ignore what they cannot understand. Others make things up. Josh wants neither. He wants admiration and worship.

  2. 07:05 Ezekiel by the Kebar: heavens opened

    Scripture: Ezekiel 1:1-3 Jeremiah 29:4-7 Jeremiah 29:10 Matthew 13:1-9 2 Corinthians 10:5

    • He reads Ezekiel 1:1-3. Thirtieth year, fourth month, fifth day, among the exiles by the Kebar. Heavens opened, visions of God. Fifth year of Jehoiachin’s exile. Word came to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi. The hand of the Lord was on him.
    • Author of Ezekiel? Ezekiel. Like Jeremiah and Zechariah, a priest. Name means God will strengthen or harden. Those three the only prophet-priests, spokesmen to exiles in Babylon. Rebuke sin, expose idolatry, reveal glorious future. Called at thirty from priest to prophet.
    • Brad last week got him thinking of men who marked him: Joe, Dan, Bob, preachers like John or Voddie. Ezekiel born 622 BC, grew up listening to Jeremiah. Daniel and Ezekiel likely knew each other before exile.
    • Jeremiah said seventy years. Settle, raise families, plant gardens. False prophets shaved years off. Changing minds is hard. Parable of the sower: pull weeds before planting. 2 Corinthians 10: demolish arguments. Twofold ministry: judgment on defiant Judah and nations, then encouragement that God would restore his people and the glory of his kingdom.

    1. 10:53 Read the vision. AI cannot paint it

      Scripture: Ezekiel 1:4-21 Ezekiel 1:22-28

      • Before the work, a detailed vision of God’s glory unlike Exodus or Isaiah. Disclaimer: he will read it all. Embrace it. Our God is awesome. Close your eyes if you want.
      • He reads Ezekiel 1:4-21: windstorm from the north, cloud, lightning, glowing metal, four living creatures, four faces, four wings, calf feet, burnished bronze, wheels intersecting wheels, rims full of eyes.
      • Millennial who ran Ezekiel 1 through an AI image creator, twenty prompts, none fully honored the text. That is the point. Nothing can represent what Ezekiel saw.
      • He finishes 1:22-28: vault like crystal, wings like rushing waters, throne of lapis lazuli, figure like a man, fire, rainbow. This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the Lord. Ezekiel fell face down.

  3. 16:38 God is still on the throne

    Scripture: Psalm 74:1-2 Genesis 12:1-3 Isaiah 6:5 Genesis 32:24-31 Exodus 34:29-35 Daniel 10:8-9 2 Corinthians 12:7 Matthew 26:39 Mark 12:30-31

    • Exile mindset in Psalm 74: why have you rejected us? Remember Abraham. Were they still chosen? Did he not punch Pharaoh? Jerusalem under enemy control, temple robbed. First lesson: no matter how bad, God is still on the throne. He has never failed a promise.
    • Where is God in the darkest moments? On his throne. At least twenty-five times Ezekiel says like and likeness. Glory is unpaintable. Kavod means weight. Eyes on the wheels: omniscience. Pair with omnipresence. If you want a God you can figure out, you will have to make one up. Ocean in a flask.
    • Ezekiel fell face down, then heard. Humble, surrendered, worship. If God works anything good it starts face down. Isaiah: woe is me. Jacob’s limp, Moses’ veil, Daniel fainted, Paul’s thorn. You have seen glory if you have been knocked down a peg.
    • Before glory: what have you done for me lately. Churches full of people who want the gift, not him. After: pray because they love to talk, read to be taught, obey to please. God glorifies God through you. Gethsemane: your will be done. Greatest command: love God, love neighbor.

  4. 24:53 Cherubim, wheels, vault, throne, bow

    Scripture: Ezekiel 10:20 Genesis 3:24 Exodus 25:18-22 1 Kings 6:23-28 Revelation 4:6-8 Genesis 9:8-17 2 Corinthians 5:21

    • Chapter 10 identifies the creatures as cherubim. Genesis 3: east of Eden, flaming sword. Tabernacle curtains, mercy seat, Solomon’s temple, Revelation. Not tooth-fairy: more Hawkman, Apache mobility. Faces: man, lion, ox, eagle—highest of their kinds. Stand in awe of sovereignty. Christ: incarnation, kingship, suffering servant, divinity. Genesis 9 covenant with man, birds, livestock, wild animals. God remembers his promise. He also uses creation to bless or discipline. Cherubim go from guarding the tree of life to ushering the redeemed because he who knew no sin became sin for us.

    1. 30:06 The wheels of the throne chariot

      Scripture: Psalm 18:10 Habakkuk 2:4

      • Not mystical tech. Glory in motion, judgment and redemption. Psalm 74 people wondered if Yahweh could make the trip from Jerusalem to Babylon. God’s throne has sick wheels. Wherever the Spirit goes. You have battles; there is no road too rough. Psalm 18: he mounted the cherubim and flew. Spinners joke. Omnidirectional sovereignty. Eyes: providence, never late. He sees injustice, secret faithfulness, lonely tears. The throne is not parked. One day it rolls up at the restoration of all things. The righteous live by faith.

    2. 34:35 Vault, throne, and a war bow laid down

      Scripture: Romans 11:33-36 Genesis 9:13 Romans 8:1 Matthew 25:21 Isaiah 53:2-3 Philippians 2:7 John 4:24 Revelation 7:12

      • Vault like crystal over the cherubim, noise like rushing waters, active providence. Throne: omnipotence, azure, fire, rainbow. The man on the throne probably a pre-incarnate appearance of Jesus. People in exile, nation about to be destroyed, God still on the throne. I got this. Israel was not a victim; God allowed Babylon to discipline those he loves. Medes and Persians, Cyrus lets them return. Romans 11 doxology.
      • Rainbow in 1:28. After the flood Noah sacrifices; God sets his bow in the clouds. Genesis 9 uses a weapon word. Josh brings a bow on stage. God hates sin; arrows of wrath. Laying the bow in the clouds means peace. Experiment pointing the bow; the room gets uneasy. Heaven’s bow points up: I will take the arrows myself. He sent his Son. No condemnation in Christ. Well done, clothed in his righteousness.
      • They will not shake this thought all series. Jesus had the glory and Isaiah says no beauty; he made himself nothing so we might share his glory. Worship in spirit and truth. Join heaven: amen, praise, glory, wisdom, thanks, honor, power, strength to our God forever.
