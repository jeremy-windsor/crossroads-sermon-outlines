Overview

  1. 00:09 Happy 2026, six-ten, and Kevin in the water

    • Brad greets the church. Two questions: he is six foot ten, not six-seven; yes basketball, yes doorways, no tall-person club. Chris stood on a chair with a measuring stick so the light would hit his head.
    • He has served as an elder five years, three kids, sixteen years married. Josh and Kristen invited their family to Circles; Kristen snagged them on a 2018 visit in front of the tech team. Josh pressed him to become an elder when Brad said he was not qualified, then urged him to preach.
    • Kevin, a college football player at Brad’s Colorado Springs gym with Eric: athletic, great attitude, military kid, tough childhood. Week before college he invited them to his baptism at sunset by Pikes Peak. He thanked a friend, a coach, two teachers, a teammate, a pastor, a family member. None of those seven were there. He would not have been in the water without each of them seeing Christ in them. Today: the impact of one person who helps, shares, and saves another.

  2. 05:40 Nazareth loves the scroll, then tries to throw him off a cliff

    Scripture: Luke 4:1-13 Deuteronomy 8:3 Luke 4:14-21 Isaiah 61:1-2 Luke 4:22-30 1 Kings 17:8-16

    • Luke 4: Jesus led into the wilderness, tempted three times—bread, kingdoms, temple. He answered from Deuteronomy, Moses reminding Israel who God is. Returned to Galilee, taught, praised. In Nazareth he took Isaiah’s scroll and declared himself anointed.
    • He reads Luke 4:18-21: good news to the poor, freedom, sight, the year of the Lord’s favor. Today this Scripture is fulfilled. They spoke well of him. Isn’t this Joseph’s son? He cuts them off: physician heal yourself; no prophet accepted in his hometown. Elijah sent to a widow in Zarephath, not Israel’s widows. Elisha cleansed none of Israel’s lepers, only Naaman the Syrian. The synagogue became furious, drove him to the brow of the hill to throw him off. He walked through the crowd.
    • First reaction: amazement. Then fury at a Gentile getting healed. Who is this Naaman, and why did first-century Jews try to kill Jesus for naming him?

  3. 10:04 Naaman, a girl who speaks, and servants who will not stay quiet

    Scripture: 2 Kings 5:1-14

    • 2 Kings 5: Naaman, commander of Aram near Damascus, Gentile enemy, raider who takes captives. Great man, valiant, but leprosy. A young girl from Israel serves his wife: if only my master would see the prophet in Samaria. She is trying to help him after what his army did. The king of Aram sends a letter and a fortune in silver, gold, and clothes. Israel’s king tears his robes: am I God? Elisha: have the man come, he will know there is a prophet in Israel.
    • Naaman stops at Elisha’s door. A messenger: wash seven times in the Jordan. He leaves angry. He wanted the prophet to come out, call on the Lord, wave a hand. Abana and Pharpar are better. Rage over extra work—like dishes beside the sink, slow walkers, loud chewers, no turn signal. Servants: my father, if he had told you a great thing would you not have done it? He dips seven times. Flesh like a young boy.

    1. 13:44 The girl who should have wanted him isolated

      • Two people jump out. First, the wife’s servant: captured, family maybe dead, village destroyed, dignity gone. You might blame the raiders, Naaman’s family, even God. Then he gets the disease that isolates, unclean, outside the camp, early death. Fair punishment? Her perspective is the opposite. If only my master would see the prophet. Unshakable: her God, not his gods, can heal even someone her sinner heart might not want healed. She does not stay quiet or celebrate misery. It bursts out to her mistress.
      • Second, Naaman’s own servants, likely from his nation, warriors. Correction would not be tolerated. They still speak when the boss’s ego and anger miss an easy cure. Six piled-up insults: disease, 100 miles into enemy land, 900 pounds of gifts, the king’s scene, a messenger not the prophet, a dirty river 30 more miles. Mental makeup depends on the foundation built in Christ. Naaman had no relationship until the Jordan. The girl responded with faith. Servants appealed to his love of great things, then simple correction. They put into action what it means to be the one for somebody by believing the One.

  4. 20:08 Speak up. God qualifies the called

    Scripture: James 5:16 Acts 16:16-34 James 5:19-20 Genesis 1:26-27 Matthew 10:19-20 Matthew 18:3

    • Do we silently celebrate when someone gets what they deserve? Whisper instead of speak? Watch a wrong decision? James 5:16 confess and pray. Paul and Silas in Acts 16: spirit out of a fortune teller, beaten, prison, hymns, jailer and household baptized. James 5:19-20: turn a wanderer, save from death, cover a multitude of sins.
    • Early on Brad feared saying the wrong thing. God does not call the qualified; he qualifies the called. Unique stories are why we are qualified to share mercy. The devil plants doubt. You may be the only Bible somebody ever reads. Treat them differently, give grace when justice would suffice, strength in adversity. Before they ask why: because Jesus first did it for me. Matthew 10:19-20: do not worry what to say; the Spirit of your Father. It is not up to you what they do with the truth. Have the hard conversation in love. Share testimony. Pray physically.
    • A stressed Monday, merging onto the 101 east from I-17. Ken Alexander calls at 8 a.m.: Reed and I were praying; your name was on our hearts. Brad told the truth about the investment meeting. Ken prayed over the phone. He believes the One until it pours out.
    • Matthew 18:3: become like little children. Forest Frank’s Jesus Is Coming Back Soon stuck in the house. Six-year-old Brevin asked a classmate if she gets it stuck in her head. Who’s Jesus? Pretty much the most important person who ever lived. Can I take a Bible to school? Thanksgiving tablecloth: host wrote Brevin’s school friend would come to know Jesus. Six-year-olds recruit others.

  5. 29:06 Believe the One, then be the one

    Scripture: John 16:9 Genesis 1:26 Isaiah 53:5 Isaiah 52:14 Luke 23:34 1 Corinthians 15:14-20 John 3:16

    • Created in God’s image for fellowship. Sin entered through Eve and Adam. Sin is anything in heart or life that refuses to trust and love God above all. John 16:9 NLT: convicted of sin because they do not believe in me. Born into sin, hell as punishment. But God sent his Son. Genesis 1:26: let us make mankind. Tempted more than we, never sinned. Flogged with embedded leather; Isaiah: pierced, crushed; Hebrew for wounds is open bleeding stripes. Crown of thorns, purple robe, 75–100 pound beam, form marred beyond likeness. Sin disfigures what God made good. Father forgive them. If he stayed dead, preaching is useless. 1 Corinthians 15: he has been raised. Paul, Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Salome, Mary mother of James, Cleopas, Thomas, James, the Twelve, more than 500. Forty days, then right hand until he returns. He cannot make us believe. Whoever believes will not perish.
    • Impact lives even if you miss the baptism. Pray in person and on the phone. Share with enemies. Correct bosses. Share Jesus like a six-year-old. First believe the One, then be the one. Write two names: the person you credit for sitting here, and the person the Spirit brought to mind whom you have not yet reached. Be one of Kevin’s seven. Unique sphere of influence. Respond to the Spirit. Prayer: confidence to speak, lives that are Bibles worth reading. Why are they patient and kind? Because of you.
