  1. 00:43 From attending to belonging

    Scripture: 1 Corinthians 13:1 1 Peter 4:10-11 1 Corinthians 6:19 1 Corinthians 10:31 Romans 12:1 John 4:24

    • Thanks for choosing Crossroads. Hope you step from attending to belonging. If this is not home and these are not family, you miss the point. The goal is to feed the Word so you mature and serve. If you have no place here to put others first, you are not mature. Organized theology without active love is noise. Much sound, not enough sacrifice.
    • 1 Peter 4: use whatever gift to serve others. Speak as God’s words; serve with God’s strength. Teaching, preaching, prophecy, wisdom, faith, healing, miracles, tongues biblically, leadership, shepherding, evangelism, giving as if handing it to God. Elders want to equip you to help, not watch. Do not be prideful: gifts exist because of rebirth. Temples of the Spirit. Also offer natural skills. Every part from nursery to Silver Bells. Hospitality, encouragement, listening, needs, forgiveness. Most ministry is unseen. Heaven sees when you walk out and when you are alone. That is the unseen church.
    • Romans 12: in view of mercy, living sacrifice, true worship. Corporate and personal. We watered true worship by letting style beat unity. Traditional, charismatic, Baptist, contemporary—he could worship with all. Independent not because they cannot make friends but because they value unity. Back-row Baptist Chris with dancing Tom from Bethel. A 70-year-old in Lululemon: Victory in Jesus then Praise. Be comfortable in spirit and truth—and keep worship in truth when you leave, or what you do here is a lie.

  2. 09:20 Fourteen months later: glory and an idol

    Scripture: Ezekiel 8:1 Exodus 40:34-38 1 Samuel 4:21-22 1 Kings 8:10-11 Ezekiel 8:2-4 Jeremiah 29:4-7 Hebrews 12:2

    • Ezekiel 8:1. Sixth year, sixth month, fifth day. Elders of Judah sitting before him. Fourteen months after chapter 1. High-level: Israel and nations had temples and priests; only Israel had the glory dwelling. Exodus 40 glory moved in; 1 Samuel 4 departed; 1 Kings 8 filled Solomon’s temple; Ezekiel watched glory leave and begin with glory on the move. Without presence, they are like any people.
    • Vision runs chapters 8–11. Goal is not to get through the text but the text through you. True worship implies false. He reads 8:2-4: figure like fire and glowing metal, grabbed by the hair, north gate, idol that provokes jealousy, glory of Israel as in the plain.
    • One would imagine Ezekiel settling in Babylon as Jeremiah said. Christians sing God is always on time then complain he is late. Christianity is not a series of dramatic visions. It is fourteen months of faithfulness. Sometimes quiet; sometimes dragged by the hair. He is not physically in Jerusalem. Fix your eyes on Jesus. Same chariot-throne, likely pre-incarnate Christ. Greatest need is not always a new vision; sometimes he renews an old call because you wandered.

  3. 14:32 The idol of jealousy and syncretism

    Scripture: Deuteronomy 32:21 2 Kings 21:1-9 John 14:6 Ezekiel 8:5-6

    • Two things at the north gate by the king’s palace: the idol that provokes jealousy and the glory. Idolatry provokes like a spouse. Deuteronomy 32: they made me jealous by what is no god. Take away sin and presence comforts; leave it and presence threatens. Thank Jesus for peace with God.
    • Leaders mixed Jehovah with pagan worship. Demoted him to one of many. Fury without pity is still a way back. Marvel at mercy. Hezekiah removed idols; Manasseh put Asherah in the house, later converted and removed it; Josiah burned and crushed; a successor likely set another at the north gate. Without glory, a nice building.
    • Syncretism: mix religions. Today: consumer Christianity, churches as brands. The pope declaring all paths—false; Jesus is the only way. Politics: searching for the cross, seeing a flag. Prosperity gospel treats suffering as lack of faith. True disciples take joy in suffering then glory. American Christians are not persecuted; stop saying they are. If the time comes, ready.

    1. 22:05 A jealous God has a right

      Scripture: Exodus 20:4-6 Isaiah 42:8

      • Exodus 20, commandment two: no images; I am a jealous God. Why is jealousy a vice in us and a virtue in him? We are not like him. Hovering over Kristen in the lobby would be control, not virtue. But if she found an affair she has a right to jealousy. Covenant promises. If God said you do you, he would deny his deity. He will share his glory with no one. If you want me plus that, you do not get me.

  4. 25:08 Through the hole: three more detestable things

    Scripture: Exodus 30:7-9 Ezekiel 8:13-14 Ezekiel 8:15-16 Deuteronomy 4:19-20 Ezekiel 8:17-18

    • Dig the wall. Seventy elders, Jaazaniah son of Shaphan, censers, crawling things, unclean animals, idols. The Lord does not see us; the Lord has forsaken the land. Exodus 30 and Leviticus 10 have rules for the censer. Seventy elders and not one said burn it. Be careful who you call elder. Growing up in church does not save. Shaphan’s house served Jeremiah; Jaazaniah burns incense to Charlotte the spider. Legacy faith is not saving faith unless it becomes yours.
    • Women mourning Tammuz, Sumerian vegetation god, fertility cult, Ishtar, winter and spring. Do not be too judgmental; we have ridiculous cults. Isaiah condemned the same. They trusted fertility gods for harvest. Elders in the dark influence the courtyard. Are you leading people to Jesus or to idols?
    • Inner court: about twenty-five men, backs to the temple, faces east, bowing to the sun. Private elder sin becomes public. Deuteronomy 4: do not bow to sun, moon, stars. I brought you out of the iron furnace; why souvenirs? Putting the branch to their nose—thumbing. They do not care. God will keep covenant even if they will not. They refused to listen; now he will not.

  5. 33:18 Would we find things that make God jealous?

    Scripture: Genesis 3:15 Revelation 13:1-18 Revelation 14:1 1 John 5:21 John 2:19-21 Romans 8:17

    • Twenty-six hundred years later, still not above the rebuke. You probably do not incense a lizard, but if we looked through a divine hole? Public devotion, private lie. Crush the snake’s head. Browser history. Worship of creation as rival priority. The question is not whether you have idols but what you will do now. God does not need you to open the closet. If Jesus cracked the skies, what are you worried about leaving? Not shame: only what is exposed can be healed. Repentance is burning them down. Inner Josiah.
    • Revelation 12–14: unholy trinity, mark of the beast versus seal of the Lamb. Those marked by the beast spared the beast’s wrath, face the Lamb’s. Those marked by the Lamb spared the Lamb, face the beast. No unmarked forehead. Which wrath can you endure?
    • Idolatry costs more than repentance. He turns enemies into friends. Jesus forsaken so we could be forgiven. Trace the temple: Babylon, Rome, Jerusalem destroyed God’s temple on a tree. You are temples of the Spirit. Keep yourselves from idols. Lay it down before bread and juice. Ezekiel 8 shows why we must be judged. The gospel: the believer is a living temple from which God will never depart. Jesus the true temple torn down and raised. Presence is comfort. If we share his sufferings we share his glory.
