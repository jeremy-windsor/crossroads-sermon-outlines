Overview

  1. 00:45 A simple shepherd, not a growth model

    Scripture: Luke 22:20 2 Corinthians 5:15 Matthew 16:24-25 1 Corinthians 6:19-20

    • Josh prays the sheep would hear, wanderers come home, stubbornness laid down. He is simple-minded, not a big goal-setter. A 500-adult goal could miss the brokenhearted widow at 490. He wants to be a faithful steward, light a fire to love God and others, a shepherd heaven can rejoice in. When you think of him: sincere love for Christ. You may dislike his eschatology, Calvinism, or style; you will not argue what God has done. Souls, not seats. No book. Fallen world at war with God. You deserve death; he defeated it. Without Jesus, condemned, stuck like Rahab needing covenant with Yeshua.
    • Luke 22: new covenant in my blood. Come on his terms: repentance, faith in finished work, submission—confess Jesus is Lord. Not a negotiated contract. Great exchange: he takes guilt, gives his righteousness. 2 Corinthians 5: died for all so the living live for him. Cannonball or never jump. Deny yourself, take up the cross. Bought at a price. Forgiveness, adoption, Spirit, sealed life. They do not want a fast-food church. Hard things, not skinny-jeans self-help. Afraid of disappointing God, not a thinning crowd.

    1. 08:46 Old Testament metaphors lean toward Jesus

      Scripture: Luke 24:27 1 Corinthians 15:45 1 Corinthians 5:7 John 3:14-15 Psalm 23:1-6

      • Ezekiel 33 and 34 will form biblical expectations of leaders. Everyone shepherds someone. Imitate the good shepherd. The Spirit lets us read ancient text the same way. Struggle with the OT? Luke 24: Moses and the prophets point to him. Not a surprise guest after 400 years of darkness.
      • Adam sleeps, bride from his side; last Adam sleeps in death, bride from the pierced side. Passover lamb; Christ our Passover. Bronze serpent; Son of Man lifted up. David slays Goliath without Saul’s armor; greater son of David with a cross. About a hundred times God calls himself a shepherd. Shepherds smell like sheep. Psalm 23. The Bible is about God coming to find us.

  2. 13:09 Watchmen, sheep, and woe to self-feeding shepherds

    Scripture: Acts 20:26-27 Ezekiel 33:11 Ezekiel 33:17 Romans 9:20 Ezekiel 34:1-6 Romans 13:1-4

    • Week two: watchman. God holds leaders accountable. Acts 20: innocent of blood, whole counsel. If heaven, also hell; holiness and sin; marriage and what is not. Ezekiel 33:11: I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked. Turn, why will you die? Not a cruel dictator flexing—he leaves the throne and sheds tears. Verse 17: you say the way of the Lord is not just. Romans 9: who are you to talk back?
    • Sheep are dumb, stubborn, walk in their own mess, helpless. Best metaphor for the human race. Title shepherd is for all leaders: family, church, state. Romans 13. Parents, pastors, politicians. Without leaders nothing gets done. Outside sheep eat first and block the rest.
    • Ezekiel 34: woe to shepherds who only take care of themselves. Eat curds, wear wool, slaughter choice animals; do not strengthen, heal, bind, bring back, search. Ruled harshly; scattered; food for wild animals.

    1. 20:39 Bad shepherds, Wycliffe, and spiritual abuse

      Scripture: Ephesians 5:25 1 John 4:1 1 Timothy 5:19-20

      • Fallen world always has bad shepherds. Sheep submit; shepherds care—like wife and husband, Christ and church. Constitution as extra-biblical shepherd-sheep: of, by, for the people. Lincoln. Sometimes sheep submit and shepherds exploit. Heart of bad leadership: they only care about themselves. Bethel headlines are not new. Shepherds are sheep with a staff.
      • Corruption hates criticism. John Wycliffe called out clergy wealth; decades after death they dug up bones, burned them, scattered ashes. Refuse to stay quiet. If you are a victim of spiritual abuse—false words, manipulation, sexual or emotional abuse, silence—Josh offers himself. Sorry. Sometimes the way to heal from a bad shepherd is a good one. Crossroads will not lord it over you. Those leaders will answer to God. Test the spirits. Do not assume every leader is corrupt. 1 Timothy 5: two or three witnesses, then rebuke before all.

  3. 27:32 I myself will search. One shepherd, my servant David

    Scripture: Ezekiel 34:7-10 Ezekiel 34:11-16 Ezekiel 34:23-24 Matthew 1:1 John 10:7-18 Mark 10:42-45 Hebrews 13:17 Hebrews 4:16 Ezekiel 48:35 Luke 15:20

    • I am against the shepherds. Leadership is stewardship, not ownership. When bad shepherds fail, God does not abandon the flock. I myself will search, rescue, gather, pasture, bind, strengthen, destroy the sleek, shepherd with justice. Jeremiah and Isaiah too. I will place over them one shepherd, my servant David. David died about 970 BC; Ezekiel begins 593; David dead about 375 years. Promise alive. Forever king. Matthew’s genealogy: son of David is Jesus.
    • John 10 read in full: I am the gate, the good shepherd, I lay down my life, other sheep, one flock. He is not offering comfort. He is announcing identity: I am Yahweh. Hired hands run. Ezekiel: bad shepherds take. Jesus: good shepherd gives. Mark 10: not so with you; servant of all; ransom for many.
    • Four ways leadership goes sideways: power without love; position without presence; vision without sacrifice; followers without responsibility. Passive sheep damage too. Hebrews 13: submit so their work is joy. You need the church. Sometimes someone must tie your legs and carry you home. Distance in sin is the devil’s shame tool. Come to the throne of grace. Jehovah Shammah: the Lord is there. What Ezekiel saw in promise, John sees in glory. The gate is open. Help us be good shepherds.
