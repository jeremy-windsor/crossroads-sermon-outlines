Overview

  1. 00:00 A valley of very dry bones

    Scripture: Ephesians 4:3 Ezekiel 37:1-14 Deuteronomy 28:25-26 2 Timothy 2:13

    • Seahawks green and Patriots colors: keep the unity of the Spirit. Series recap: throne room, canal with weeping exiles, hole in the wall at Jerusalem, now a valley of dry bones. Looking to the end of the age and a dead nation raised. Resurrection before they had the name.
    • He reads Ezekiel 37:1-14. Can these bones live? Sovereign Lord, you alone know. Object lesson on bodily resurrection. Shrek layers: the vision is Israel’s return, God bringing dead people to life nationally, spiritually, eternally. Then, now, future. Bones likely slain soldiers, humiliating unburied Jewish death. Deuteronomy 28 carcass curse. Though we are unfaithful, he remains faithful.
    • Not fresh bones. Netflix true-crime dry. Prophesy: breath, tendons, flesh, skin. Rattling, bone to bone, no breath. Prophesy to the breath from the four winds. A vast army. These bones are the whole house of Israel: hope gone, cut off. I will open your graves, put my Spirit in you, settle you in your land.

  2. 08:46 Then: exile graves. Also: human exile from the tree

    Scripture: Romans 4:17 Genesis 12:3 Genesis 2:9 Romans 5:12 Genesis 3:24 Romans 6:23 Revelation 22:1-3 John 3:8 Ephesians 2:1-5 John 20:22

    • From a human view, no. Heavenly: why not? Romans 4: God who gives life to the dead. Israel losing Abraham’s hope that all peoples would be blessed. Cut off means die. If that language does not drive you to Calvary, you missed the magnitude.
    • Historical: they feel nationally dead, not spiritually. Return under Zerubbabel, Ezra, Nehemiah. Also a bigger exile: Adam and Eve from Eden. Tree of life in the middle. We were not created to die. Romans 5: sin entered, death through sin. Trace the tree: Genesis 2 sanctuary, Genesis 3 cherubim, menorah, Jesus hung on a tree, veil torn, firstfruits, Eucharist as fruit of the true tree, Revelation 22 both sides of the river. Begins, ends, and is connected by a tree at Golgotha, place of the skull.
    • Valley of bones to place of the skull. Four winds into the lungs of Jesus. Lazarus rule: he would die again. Return from exile still left them spiritually dead. John 3 wind; Ephesians 2 dead, but God made us alive. Ruach. John 20: he breathed, receive the Holy Spirit. Death becomes a doorman. Lion King theology is wrong. Sister died before 40.

    1. 22:55 Ask them for an alternative to the empty tomb

      Scripture: Matthew 19:25-26 Romans 8:10-11 Ezekiel 36:25-27 John 3:5-6 1 Peter 1:3-5

      • No one debates the tomb is empty. Eyewitnesses touched, ate, spoke, marched to death. Church born in the city where you could drive past the tomb. Provide the body. Not visions or drugs. Watergate: liars crack. If only spiritual or metaphorical or in hearts, the tomb would have a body. God redeems the physical. If Christ is not raised, neither are we. Christianity stands or falls here.
      • Who then can be saved? With God all things are possible. Main problem is not dryness. It is death. Dead people do not swim to rescue. Hear the truth, be born again, believe. God sent a preacher to bones. Rattling because the word was spoken. Still lifeless without ruach. Romans 8: Spirit who raised Jesus will give life to mortal bodies. Ezekiel 36: sprinkle water, new heart, heart of flesh, my Spirit in you. John 3: born of water and the Spirit. Baptism displays what already happened. 1 Peter 1 living hope. You are the bones, not Ezekiel. Dry bones, hear the word. Jesus took the covenant curse. Cut off so we could be welcomed in.

  3. 36:50 Watch what I can do

    Scripture: John 5:25-29 Luke 24:5-6 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17 1 Corinthians 15:26 1 Corinthians 15:54-57 Ephesians 5:14

    • Picture bleached silent bones. Can these bones live hangs over every cemetery and ICU. You alone know. Watch my greatest wonder. He does not inspire bones. He raises them. John 5: the dead will hear and live; all in the graves will come out. Virgin-born Son of Man, three days cold, breath entered, he walked out. Why look for the living among the dead? Dead in Christ rise first. Lowly bodies like his. Last enemy destroyed. Death swallowed up in victory. If this is not true, nothing matters. If it is, everything matters. Some feel like these bones. Same voice speaking now. Revival cannot be programmed. Wake up, sleeper. He has spoken and will make good. Pray: last chance to sing, eat, praise.
