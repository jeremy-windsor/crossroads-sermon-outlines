Overview

  1. 00:32 Hope and holiness go together

    Scripture: 1 John 3:2-3

    • Forging Faith is a simple two-part series. Last week, the first half of 1 Peter 1, walking in hope. They closed singing victory in Jesus. This week, the back half: walking in holiness. Peter, John, and every New Testament writer would argue the two go together.
    • John: now we are children of God; when he appears we shall be like him. All who have this hope purify themselves as he is pure. Holding the hope that Jesus returns to right every wrong is a purifying hope.
    • Holy, hagios, root meaning different. Bent: formed, then bent, never the same. Holy people are not weird. They are reshaped, set apart, different from their old life and from unbelieving neighbors. That bent surprises people with no hope.

    1. 02:23 The world recruits; aliens struggle to fit

      Scripture: 1 Peter 4:3-5 1 John 2:15-17

      • 1 Peter 4: you spent enough time doing what pagans choose. They are surprised you do not join reckless living and heap abuse on you. They will give account to him who judges the living and the dead.
      • The church has failed to be honest about how hard holiness is. As the gospel wins souls it also exposes an anti-God segment Scripture calls the world: not a passive you-do-you force but an active recruiter led by Satan, seeking to devour.
      • 1 John 2: do not love the world. Cravings, lust of the eyes, boasting come from the world. The world passes away; whoever does the will of God lives forever.
      • Last week Peter called us aliens. Being different is hard. Josh and Kristen watched a Netflix comedy about an alien trying to fit in, watching Grey’s Anatomy to learn doctoring, missing sarcasm. Resident aliens struggle in a fallen world. Alien speaks to future hope. Another title speaks more to holiness: stone.

  2. 05:21 From ordinary stone to precious stone

    Scripture: John 1:42 Revelation 21:14 Revelation 21:19 1 Peter 2:4-5

    • John 1: Simon son of John will be called Cephas, Peter, stone. Revelation 21: the wall had twelve foundations, the names of the twelve apostles; foundations decorated with every kind of precious stone. Ordinary Cephas becomes a precious foundation stone. God is a promise keeper and a promise exceeder. The promise to Peter alone expands: all the apostles, then all believers.
    • 1 Peter 2: as you come to the living stone, rejected by humans, chosen and precious, you also like living stones are being built into a spiritual house, a holy priesthood. A gem does not reach potential until it reflects light. The light of Christ makes the beauty visible.

    1. 07:44 Joshua’s twelve stones, Jesus’ twelve foundations

      Scripture: Joshua 4:1-9 Revelation 21:12

      • Joshua 4: memorial at Gilgal, twelve riverbed stones. Jordan at the front of the promise; crystal river at fulfillment. Greater Joshua builds a city of twelve precious stones. Gates are the twelve tribes. All God’s people collected. Who thought being called aliens and stones would become the joy of the church?

  3. 08:49 Gird up. Be holy. Love covers.

    Scripture: 1 Peter 1:13 2 Corinthians 10:5 1 Peter 4:7-8 1 Peter 5:8-9

    • 1 Peter 1:13: therefore, with minds alert and fully sober. Josh says the ESV is better here: preparing your minds for action. Greek: girding up your loins. Robes hiked into the belt. Modern: roll up sleeves, lace boots, get your head in the game.
    • Fully sober is not only about drink. Paul: take thoughts captive. Peter: sober-minded. Josh’s paraphrase: do not be ignorant of your surroundings. Observe everything, admire nothing. Christ’s return should make us cool and collected. Many get lost in end-times studies and lose their cool. Peter’s eschatology: the end of all things is near; therefore be alert and sober so you may pray. Above all, even above eschatology, love each other deeply, because love covers a multitude of sins.
    • Sober also means knowing Satan prowls like a roaring lion. Resist, stand firm; fellow believers worldwide suffer the same. Satan loves to kill alertness with boredom. Lazy Christians have no outward-facing ministry, only an echo chamber. Chirp, little dirt under the nails.

    1. 11:59 Be holy because I am holy

      Scripture: 1 Peter 1:13-16 Job 38:4-7 Isaiah 55:9 Romans 9:20-21 Matthew 16:25-26

      • Set hope on the grace to be brought when Jesus is revealed. As obedient children do not conform to former evil desires. Be holy in all you do, for it is written, be holy because I am holy. When God explained who he was to Israel, he explained first that he is holy, different. They forgot and prostituted themselves to created things.
      • We are in his image, not like him. Job: where were you when I placed the stars? His thoughts as far above as the moon. Romans 9: who are you to talk back to God? Pottery does not ask the potter about the handle. He is holy. Think critically, then say you are God, I am not. Anything good in you is because of him.
      • Jesus: whoever wants to save their life will lose it; what good to gain the world and forfeit the soul. You lived one way; now live his way. Do not go back. Children inherit a parent’s nature. If kids drive you crazy it might be you. Live with Jesus long enough and you sound like him. Immature believers who do not look like him are not spending time with him. True salvation results in obedience, not perfection, but a progressive bend.

  4. 18:45 Grade your devotion

    Scripture: Psalm 47:1 Psalm 35:27 1 Timothy 2:8 1 John 1:5 1 Corinthians 10:31

    • Quiet 1 through 10. If you said 10 or even 9: is that in time, money, weekends? Does the wallet confirm the testimony? Last week about 400 adults, maybe 230 households. 90 percent of giving from 80 units. If everyone tithed, average household income would be $27,000. 46 percent of the church carries 90 percent of the financial burden. The Lord knows who carries the weight. God is not number one on a list. He is not in the same hemisphere as the rest of the list.
    • Birthday-month joke: Kristen did not sign up to be number one girl but only girl. No one has loved you like God. Why search for reasons to love him less?
    • Worship team volunteers; people stare like teenagers. Scripture: clap your hands, shout. Some cannot show up on time. He was late himself. Psalm 35: those who delight in my righteousness shout for joy. 1 Timothy: men everywhere pray, lifting holy hands. Prayer and worship connect as hope and holiness do. Christian homes are a mess because men treat Jesus as a side hustle and force women to be priest in the home. He thought he lost his marriage once. With Jesus anything is possible. Men, drag your family to the altar. Without Jesus you are a non-impressive stone. With him, precious. God is light. Whatever you cannot do for his glory, do not do.

  5. 26:01 Redeemed with precious blood, not silver

    Scripture: 1 Peter 1:17-21 John 19:30 2 Corinthians 5:10 1 Corinthians 4:5 Deuteronomy 10:17

    • 1 Peter 1:17-21: a Father who judges impartially. Live as foreigners in reverent fear. Not redeemed with perishable silver or gold from an empty ancestral way of life, but with the precious blood of Christ, a lamb without blemish, chosen before creation, revealed in these last times. Since God is serious about sin, his children are. They will never enjoy sin like they used to because they recognize it nailed Jesus to the cross.
    • Josh chewed tobacco nearly fifteen years. At Hunter and Arena’s wedding, walk tasted like the old chew; he could not stay at the table. From avoiding the smell to being appalled. God wants to change our taste for idols. Righteousness is imputed, not a license to keep living. Judgment here is of works, not salvation. Tetelestai. The judgment seat of Christ will search actions and motives to find the good he placed. 1 Corinthians 4: wait till the Lord comes; each will receive praise from God. Some do a lot with a little; some waste a lot. Deuteronomy: no partiality, no bribes. Lineage, nationality, daddy’s money cannot save. Only the blood.

    1. 31:09 Never forget what you were: slaves ransomed

      Scripture: Proverbs 22:7 Genesis 3:21 Genesis 22:7-13 Exodus 12:1-13 Isaiah 53:7 John 1:29 Revelation 5:12 Acts 2:23-24 John 10:17-18

      • The greatest motivator for holy living is the love of God. Communion in one word: remember. Never forget what we were. Redeemed had cultural meaning in Rome, about 60 million slaves. Two options: buy yourself or a master sells you to one who frees you. Redemption was rare. If slavery was nearly impossible to escape, how do we escape sin? Option one, purchase yourself, is off the table.
      • Americans are not free. We enslaved ourselves and chose our Pharaoh. Food, alcohol, drugs, gambling, porn, debt: the borrower is slave to the lender. MasterCard nailed the branding. Jesus ransoms slaves as God crushed Pharaoh. Why go back to Egypt? He does not help you manage sin. He puts it to death. Some think this is just who I am. Heaven cheers you on. Redeem means set free by paying a price. Innocent victim for the guilty: Genesis 3 clothing, ram for Isaac, Passover lamb, Isaiah 53, Isaac’s where is the lamb, John the Baptist’s look, Revelation 5 worthy is the lamb.
      • Nothing accidental. Pentecost sermon: handed over by God’s deliberate plan and foreknowledge; you nailed him; God raised him. If heaven opened you would hear Jesus say crucify me. John 10: I lay it down of my own accord.

  6. 38:59 What hast thou given for me?

    Scripture: 1 Peter 1:22-25 James 5:14 John 8:36

    • 1858: Frances Ridley Havergal saw a crucified Christ captioned I did this for thee. What hast thou done for me? She threw the poem in the fire; it came out unharmed. The church has sung I gave my life for thee. Is there something to toss in the forge not to purify but to destroy?
    • Peter closes with sincere love, born again of imperishable seed, the word that endures. Some fight old family chains they did not create. Some wear chains they chose. Holding bread and cup is remembering freedom and renouncing slavery. Call for elders to pray and anoint. Oil is not magic; probably olive oil. Obedience invites power. Talk to Jesus: you have given so much; I will give this up. He hung the stars; he can change a taste for alcohol or weed. Come to the table as ransomed heirs, not trembling slaves. If the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.
