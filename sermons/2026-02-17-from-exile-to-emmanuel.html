Overview

  1. 00:41 If Christ met you, you want his people

    Scripture: John 10:27-30 Romans 5:8-10 1 John 2:19 Romans 10:9

    • Josh loves singing with them. He is a better husband because of Jesus. Addiction from 12 to 32; baptized at nine, rebellious at 12, faithful late 20s. Could fall any moment. Eyes on Jesus. Asks for strength, patience, rest; he never left even in rebellion. Wants a postcard; God already breathed out a letter. Word fills, directs, reminds, awakens, calms. Crazy Christians, but he has to be near them. Outpouring: serve, sing, tell someone Jesus loves them.
    • Church is not a chore. If it feels like one, consider whether you have received what you say. Mercy does not give what we deserve; grace gives more. Forgiveness, adoption, peace, eternal life, living hope, Spirit. Nobody guilt-trips a cancer survivor to their party. Fast-food churches every half hour so you can keep idols. This is the Lord’s Day, the first day, every Sunday Easter. Inspect the empty tomb together. Tell the coach Sundays are out. Less than one percent go pro. Do not make room for him; save him a seat.
    • Perseverance of the saints. Storms cannot take them off the rock. Names written with a Sharpie. John 10: no one snatches them. When you fall you slip from one hand to another. Romans 5: while we were sinners. People who walked away: they went out because they were not of us. Judas never loved Jesus; Peter restored by the fire, Judas by temple religion. Confess Jesus is Lord and believe he was raised: you are his. Grief over sin is evidence. Spend less time on leavers, more praising the one who will not let go.

  2. 13:33 Glory comes home through the east gate

    Scripture: Ezekiel 40:1-4 John 17:21

    • Last minutes in Ezekiel: foundations, gushing water, a God greater than every image. God on the move. Tabernacle, Solomon’s house, glory departed, empty temple, covenant curses, Babylon. First half judgment; second half grace, regenerate, restore; 40–48 God’s return.
    • Ezekiel 40:1-4. Twenty-fifth year, fourteen years after the city fell. High mountain, bronze man with cord and rod. Tell Israel everything you see. When glory left, everything fell apart. Now hope is renewed. Not an OT scholar—third lowest IQ in the family, one point above a child. Love Jesus; do not call someone a bad tree for a different interpretation if it is not heresy. Unity: he prayed we would be one. Read naturally, in context, not newspaper clippings.

    1. 18:17 Charity on the return: one visible coming

      Scripture: Matthew 24:34 1 Thessalonians 4:16-18 2 Thessalonians 2:1-4 John 16:33 Acts 14:22

      • Someone told him he was not saved because he does not think the rapture is biblical. Junior-high Left Behind. The Bible never describes a secret separate coming. One visible climactic event. Matthew 24 this generation: not talking past the disciples to baby boomers. Flee to the mountains, pregnant women—destruction of the temple; Christians were in the mountains. 1 Thessalonians 4 loud command; how watch the dead rise if secretly raptured? 2 Thessalonians 2 man of sin. Christians do not escape; they rise to greet the King. No two-stage return from the first century to the 1800s. Do not call a believer a heretic for thinking the church goes through tribulation. In this world you will have trouble. Paul stoned. Path to glory is suffering. Last trumpet at the end.

  3. 24:52 Glory fills the temple. Four views. Affection over agreement

    Scripture: Ezekiel 43:1-5 1 Samuel 4:21-22 Acts 1:11 Revelation 20:1-6 Galatians 3:16 Galatians 3:26 1 Corinthians 3:16-17 Revelation 22:1-5 Ezekiel 48:35 Revelation 21:22 Deuteronomy 29:29

    • Ezekiel 43: glory from the east, roar of waters, land radiant, like Kebar, fell face down, glory entered the east gate, filled the temple. 1 Samuel 4: ark captured, Eli, Ichabod. Ezekiel saw God leave; twenty years later he returns through the same gate. Acts 1: this same Jesus will come back the same way. Skip the measurements.
    • Four views. One: future literal millennial temple, Revelation 20, blueprints. Josh does not hold it; loves those who do. Called a heretic on a Sunday morning. Two: post-captivity ideal guiding Zerubbabel then Herod; measurements theological. Three: christological—temple fulfilled in Christ and the church. Not replacement; promise to the seed, Galatians 3. Are you in Christ by faith? Future for Israel through the gospel. 1 Corinthians 3 you are God’s temple. Ezekiel 47 river and Revelation 22. Why return to animal sacrifices? Four: new-earth view. Jehovah Shammah, Revelation 21 no temple because the Lord and the Lamb are the temple. Affection over agreement. Unity over uniformity. Secret things belong to the Lord. Jesus is coming back. Are you ready?

  4. 37:01 The trickle becomes a river no one can cross

    Scripture: Ezekiel 47:1-5 Ephesians 3:20-21 Ezekiel 47:8-12 Revelation 21:5 John 7:37-39 Ephesians 2:19-22

    • He did not know how to close: glory, judgment, bones, shepherds, measured temple. Then the river. Ezekiel 47: water from under the threshold, trickle, Arizona two-sprinkles. Keep walking. Ankle, knee, waist, then a river you cannot cross. Gospel flows like this. Get in a life group, show up, walk away from temptation. Immeasurably more. Flows to the Dead Sea: dead marriages, churches, sinners live. Presence unleashed into the world. New hearts, new shepherd, new covenant, new temples, new river.
    • John 7: if anyone thirsty come drink; rivers of living water, the Spirit. Feast of Tabernacles flask from Siloam. Jesus fulfills Ezekiel 47. True temple after death, resurrection, ascension. Ephesians 2: no longer foreigners, cornerstone, holy temple, dwelling of God by the Spirit. Wanderers come home. Waters ready. Trust him. Do not relegate this to a foolish argument. Swim in the depths. We want you.
