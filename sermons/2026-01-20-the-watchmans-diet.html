Overview

  1. 00:45 Covenant policemen, then a better prophet

    Scripture: Deuteronomy 27:9 Deuteronomy 27:1-29:29 Acts 3:13-25 Galatians 3:13-14 Habakkuk 2:4

    • Josh loves the song and wants to lay a foundation. Last week they approached the likeness of the throne. This entire series is about God changing hearts for his glory. Today we see his glory in the face of Christ. Prophets of old were covenant enforcement mediators, not innovators: certified spokesmen calling people back to Sinai. God remained faithful when they were not.
    • Marriage analogy: you cannot fully prepare for covenant with a flawed human. They talked faith, family, finances; nobody warned about pillows and the thermostat. Many marry to be happier; God uses it to make them holier. Israel’s covenant differed: clear blessings and curses in Deuteronomy 27–29. Prophets called a prostituted people back. They could announce judgment but not remove guilt, call repentance but not change hearts, enforce but not fulfill.
    • Jesus is the final prophet and true mediator of a better covenant. Peter in Acts 3: you killed the author of life; God raised him; repent so sins may be wiped out. Moses promised a prophet like me. Jesus bears the curses himself. Galatians 3: cursed is everyone hung on a tree so Abraham’s blessing might come to the Gentiles. Prophets called for obedience; Christ calls for faith. Obedience comes as overflow from a new heart.

  2. 09:18 I have made you a watchman

    Scripture: Ezekiel 3:17 Ezekiel 2:1-2 Ezekiel 2:3-5 Isaiah 55:10-11 John 3:19-21 Ezekiel 2:6-7 Matthew 10:28

    • Unique title in chapter 3: watchman. All offer gifts; there will always be special watchmen set apart to warn. Listen long enough and you become one. Every watchman must know: Christ left equality with God, died, rose, is coming again.
    • Son of man—humility of Christ taking a man’s title. Stand up; the Spirit raises him. You cannot teach what you have not been taught. Train the tongue by filling the ear. Last week he fell face down; we are dust; God never forgets. Humility first, then stand and take orders. Transfiguration: tents on the mountain, then come down. You cannot merely be educated in the faith; you must be altered by it.
    • Sent to a rebellious, obstinate house. Thank God he runs after rebels and does not rely on us for outcomes. Whether they listen or not, they will know a prophet has been among them. Isaiah 55: my word will not return empty. Some convinced, many refuse. John 3: light has come; people love darkness. God is not shocked. Do not be afraid of briars, thorns, scorpions. Josh’s family shuts down over a hallway scorpion. Fear drives him to God who drives fear out. If Ezekiel needed three reminders in one verse, how many more do we? Matthew 10: do not fear those who kill the body.

    1. 18:25 Eat the scroll: sweet and sour

      Scripture: Ezekiel 2:8-3:3 Revelation 10:9-10 Hebrews 12:11 John 6:35 John 6:60

      • January 18 poll on New Year diets. Start the watchman diet. Open your mouth and eat what I give you. Scroll written on both sides with lament, mourning, woe. Tasted sweet as honey. Nine-month-olds eat remotes and refuse greens. Heroes of the faith eat scrolls; problem Christians refuse to open. John eats a little scroll, honey then sour stomach. Ezekiel will too. Promises taste sweet; correction is sour. Hebrews 12: no discipline seems pleasant. Jesus is bread of life and a hard saying. Unbalanced sweet diet fattens you spiritually. If you have never tasted sour, you have never truly swallowed the word. Commit to a reading plan: esv.org, Bible app. Delete socials. Get it from the source, not podcast Christians. Test even Josh. The Lord will not bless a church full of lazy, disobedient Christians.

  3. 26:13 A forehead like flint, then sit seven days

    Scripture: John 8:11 Colossians 1:27 Ezekiel 3:5-9 Matthew 11:23-24 Ezekiel 3:14-15 Romans 12:15 Romans 9:1-4 John 15:12-13

    • Encounter the gospel before you are effective with it. Share when you fell face down. Testimony is the greatest tool. Holy Spirit: comfort and conviction, neither do I condemn you and go sin no more. Christ in you, the hope of glory. Pour him out.
    • Why fear not? Not sent to obscure speech; if sent to them they would have listened. Israel will not listen because they will not listen to me. I will make your forehead harder than flint. As hard as they are toward disobedience, you will be harder toward obedience. The church has grown soft; one rebuke and they claim church hurt. Spurgeon: bold-hearted men are always called mean-spirited by cowards. Tozer: familiarity breeds complacency. Matthew 11: if Sodom had seen Capernaum’s miracles it would have remained. Do not chase desires with stubbornness. The Christians you call mean might be God’s watchmen.
    • Truth-tellers, do not grill the lobby. Skilled watchman: Spirit lifted him; he went in bitterness and anger, then sat among the exiles at Tel Aviv seven days, deeply distressed. Sat, felt, wept before he spoke. Find a church whose pastor walks with the people, not only a screen. Weep with those who weep. Romans 9: Paul could wish himself cursed for Israel. Love one another as I have loved you.

  4. 36:19 Four outcomes, then a better covenant

    Scripture: Ezekiel 3:16-21 Romans 6:23 Galatians 5:1 Romans 9:30-32 Romans 10:4

    • I have made you a watchman. Warn the wicked or their blood is on your hands. Warn and they refuse: you saved yourself. Righteous who turn to evil without warning die; blood on you. Warn the righteous and they listen: they live, you saved yourself. Covenant: obey and be blessed, disobey and face consequences. They blamed parents, the last generation, God. Each individual will give account. If you point at those before you, they answer first; no one to point at when you stand.
    • Sit under the weight. Performance is heavy. Wages of sin is death. Enjoy this side of Calvary. Do not ignore the Old Testament; it stirs praise. It is for freedom Christ set us free. External law-righteousness versus internal faith-righteousness. Romans 9: Gentiles obtained righteousness by faith; Israel stumbled over the stone. The law exposed sin; it could not cure. Christ is the culmination of the law.
    • Ezekiel stood where blood guilt rested on the watchman. We stand under a better covenant. Jesus is the faithful watchman: he warned, wept, obeyed, took the curse. We warn not to earn life but because life has been given. Speak because Christ saved us. Live not under fear of death but in freedom.

    1. 42:29 Whoever believes will be saved

      Scripture: Mark 16:15-16 Romans 3:23 John 3:16

      • Invitation to join the family. Last words before he left were a command: go preach. Whoever believes will be saved; whoever does not will be condemned. Believe, be baptized, be saved. He spoke with grief, not anger. Love demands clarity. Reject Christ and you reject life. All have fallen short. We were enemies. Trust Jesus, receive eternal life. Waters are ready. Will you listen or refuse?
      • Prayer: thank you for making a way. Josh once stood outside, a scorpion who stung God’s people. God reached in and made a new heart. You and me, God. Help the stubborn hear, repent, believe. We come to the table ready to be changed.
